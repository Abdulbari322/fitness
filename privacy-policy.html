Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

At Securely Us, your privacy and confidentiality are at the heart of everything we do. This policy explains what personal information Rebeca Islam ("we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using this website or our services, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us — your name, email address, phone number, and any details you share when you book a consultation, complete a contact form, or correspond with us.
  • Session information — notes and records relating to your care, which are kept confidential and stored securely.
  • Technical information — basic analytics such as pages visited, browser type, and device, collected automatically to help us improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, deliver, and follow up on your sessions.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the support you request.
  • To meet our professional, ethical, and legal obligations.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve your experience.

Confidentiality

Everything you share within the therapeutic relationship is treated as confidential. We only disclose information where you have given consent, or where we are legally or ethically required to do so — for example, where there is a serious risk of harm to you or another person.

How We Protect Your Data

We use secure, HIPAA-conscious tools for video sessions and record-keeping, and we limit access to your information to what is necessary to provide your care. While no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (such as scheduling or video platforms) solely so they can perform services on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Cookies

This website uses cookies to function and to understand site usage. You can manage your preferences at any time on our Cookie Settings page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out via our contact page.
