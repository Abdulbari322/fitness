What Is Online Therapy?

The same depth of care —
wherever you are.

Online therapy offers the full range of therapeutic services through secure video sessions. You connect from your home, your office, or any private space — with no compromise in quality, depth, or the therapeutic relationship.

Research consistently shows that online therapy is equally effective as in-person therapy for the vast majority of concerns, including trauma, grief, depression, anxiety, and relationship issues. Many clients find it easier to open up in their own environment — and the removal of travel, scheduling rigidity, and the need to "show up" somewhere often makes consistent attendance more likely.

For clients living outside New York City, internationally, or those with health limitations, young children, or demanding schedules — online therapy means care that actually fits your life.

Why Online?

Online therapy works
especially well when…

✦

You live outside a major city or in a country where specialised trauma and marriage therapy is difficult to access — particularly in English.

✦

Your schedule is demanding or unpredictable — parenthood, shift work, travel — and you need flexibility that an in-person practice can't offer.

✦

Social anxiety, agoraphobia, or physical health limitations make the act of getting to an office a barrier to care that shouldn't exist.

✦

You're in a situation — living with a controlling partner, in a new city without a support network — where attending a physical clinic could feel unsafe or exposing.

✦

You simply feel more comfortable at home — and opening up in your own space feels easier than doing so in an unfamiliar office.

✦

You want continuity of care — to work with the same therapist even when you travel, relocate, or your circumstances change.
