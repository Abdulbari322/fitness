About the Specialist

Rebeca Islam,
Trauma Specialist, Accredited EP Practitioner

With over a decade working at the intersection of relational trauma and emotional recovery, I specialize in helping individuals reclaim their identity, voice, and sense of safety — after abuse, loss, or the quiet devastation of a broken heart.

My practice is built on one conviction: you are not broken. You are a person who has endured extraordinary pain, and you deserve extraordinary care in return.

The Method

A framework built
on safety first.

My approach is integrative — drawing from the most effective, trauma-informed modalities available, woven together by genuine human care.

1

Safety & Stabilisation

Before we process anything, we build the internal and external resources that make healing possible without retraumatisation.

Using EMDR, narrative therapy, and somatic approaches to gently revisit and reprocess what happened at a pace you control.

Common Questions

You may be
wondering…

There is no "ready." If part of you is curious about healing — even while the rest of you is scared — that's enough. Our first session is a gentle conversation, not a commitment. You set the pace entirely.

Trauma therapy uses specialised modalities (like EMDR) that work with the nervous system, not just the mind. This allows us to process experiences that talk therapy alone often cannot reach — memories stored in the body, not just the intellect.

Research consistently shows that online therapy is equally effective for most concerns, including trauma. Many clients find it easier to open up from their own safe space, and the flexibility removes barriers to consistent attendance — which is key to progress.

Healing doesn't have a fixed timeline and I won't pretend it does. Some clients feel significant shifts in 8–12 weeks. Others work with me for a year or more. We regularly review your progress together, and you always decide when you've reached what you came for.
