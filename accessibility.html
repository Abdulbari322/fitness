Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 20 July 2026

At Securely Us, we believe healing should be within reach for everyone. We are committed to making this website usable and welcoming for people of all abilities, and to continually improving the experience for every visitor.

Our Commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as a benchmark for accessible design. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we review our website regularly as standards and technologies evolve.

What We Do

  • Use clear, readable typography with generous spacing and strong colour contrast.
  • Provide descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Structure pages with semantic headings so they are easy to navigate.
  • Design layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Support keyboard navigation for interactive elements.

Flexible Sessions

Beyond the website, our care is designed to be accessible. Online sessions remove the need to travel, and we are glad to make reasonable adjustments — such as flexible scheduling or alternative formats — to support clients with health limitations, disabilities, or caregiving responsibilities.

Known Limitations

Some third-party tools embedded on this site, such as the booking calendar, are provided by external services and may not be fully within our control. If you encounter difficulty with any of these, please contact us and we will assist you personally.

Need Help or Have Feedback?

If you experience any barrier using this website, or you would like information in a different format, we want to hear from you. Please reach out via our contact page and we will do our best to help promptly.
